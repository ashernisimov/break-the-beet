Meet the Chef

Eric Brown

Executive Chef & Founder, Break the Beet

Eric Brown is a veteran of New York's fine dining scene, celebrated for his creative and seasonal cuisine that blends Mediterranean and New York influences. Trained at the French Culinary Institute, Eric has spent over a decade leading culinary teams across the city's premier restaurants and hospitality groups.

Having risen through the ranks at iconic venues—from Marcus Samuelsson Group to Jean-Georges' ABC Kitchen—Eric brings a modern, cross-cultural vision to every menu he creates. His talent for fusing fresh, local ingredients with classic techniques earned him leadership roles at Tao Group, Mercado Little Spain (Hudson Yards), and as Executive Chef for several renowned boutique hotels.

Eric's philosophy is simple: Food should spark joy, memories, and connection. He is passionate about personalizing the dining experience for each event—whether it's an intimate family gathering or a spectacular corporate celebration.

Credentials

  • French Culinary Institute, New York—Graduate
  • Executive Chef, ThinkFoodGroup (2022–2025)
  • Senior Sous Chef, Mercado Little Spain, Hudson Yards (2019–2022)
  • Lead roles at Marcus Samuelsson Group, Tao Group, Jean-Georges' ABC Kitchen, Mondrian Soho
