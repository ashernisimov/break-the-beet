Eric Brown

Founder & Executive Chef

Chef Eric Brown is the culinary visionary behind Break the Beet, bringing a wealth of experience from some of the world's most prestigious kitchens to intimate gatherings and private events.

With a passion for creating memorable dining experiences, Chef Brown has dedicated his career to mastering the art of personalized cuisine. His culinary journey has taken him through the kitchens of renowned establishments, where he honed his skills under the tutelage of culinary legends.

Chef Brown's philosophy centers on the belief that exceptional food is more than sustenance—it's an experience that brings people together. Each dish he creates tells a story, combining global influences with locally sourced, premium ingredients to craft menus that are as unique as the occasions they celebrate.

At Break the Beet, Chef Brown personally oversees every event, ensuring that each gathering receives the meticulous attention to detail and culinary excellence that has become his signature. His approach blends technical precision with creative innovation, resulting in dishes that are both visually stunning and unforgettable in flavor.

Culinary Training & References

  • Trained under renowned Chef Marcus Samuelsson, celebrated for his innovative approach to global cuisine and commitment to culinary excellence
  • Experience in the kitchens of Chef Jean-Georges Vongerichten, learning the art of French cuisine with Asian influences and fine dining presentation
  • Specialized training in international culinary techniques and flavor profiles
  • Expertise in menu development, dietary customization, and intimate event catering
  • Commitment to sourcing premium, seasonal ingredients and supporting local purveyors
